# Which consulting firm should you pick for AI? We spent 20 weeks building a data answer.

**Every AI program hits the same three questions: which consulting firm do we pick, how do we choose, and is any of it working?**

Most answers you can find are marketing. So we built evidence instead: an index that tracks **17 consulting and professional services firms** — MBB, the Big Four, Accenture, Capgemini, and specialist strategy houses — week by week. **277 public AI actions** logged over **20 weeks**, each mapped to nine weighted dimensions (technology partnerships, proprietary platforms, M&A, delivery, client value, governance, thought leadership…) with **a link to the original source**. Recent evidence weighs more than old evidence — standing still means slowly losing ground.

The current top five: **BCG, Deloitte, McKinsey, Accenture, PwC.** Four traits separate them from the rest of the board:

🔹 **Alliances that ship, not logos.** BCG runs joint delivery teams with Microsoft; McKinsey just allied with Cognition to bring Devin, the autonomous AI engineer, into client work. The partnerships that score are ones with delivery roles attached.

🔹 **Named platforms in production.** Deloitte's Converge agent suite, KPMG's Clara intelligence audit platform, Edisyl from Deloitte's latest acquisition. The leaders productize; the rest publish slide decks.

🔹 **Buying AI capability, not just building it.** Oliver Wyman is acquiring a 1,200-person AI consultancy; Deloitte bought Flipside's AI business. When the talent market is this tight, M&A is the shortcut.

🔹 **Research that frames the market.** Every top-five firm runs a flagship cadence — McKinsey's Global Banking Review, BCG on the AI compute shortage, PwC on quantum-era security. Research is how they set the agenda before the RFP exists.

🧮 **And the part built for you: benchmark yourself.**

The same nine-dimension model is a self-assessment tool — score your own organization 0–5 on each dimension and get a 0–100 total, plotted against the median of the 17 tracked firms on every dimension. You stop hearing "we're behind on AI" and start seeing **which dimension the gap is actually in**, with a checklist of what evidence to build first.

One more finding: the board used to be noisy — in week 2, the average firm moved 2.5 rank positions in a single week. Twenty weeks and 277 events later, recent weeks move ~0.5. **A ranking that only moves on meaningful evidence is the only kind you can act on.**

**So what:** if you're shortlisting AI consulting partners, don't compare brochure claims. Compare evidence trails — and use the same dimensions to locate where your own organization stands.

⚠️ Caveats: this is a research preview, not an authoritative ranking; it only sees public, verifiable actions; quiet ≠ doing nothing.
